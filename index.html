Supplier.Detective

A Mind that checks a supplier before a payment goes out. It reads the invoice and the purchase order, buys verified facts from Telegraph through Anjoman, double-checks anything missing or doubtful against free official sources, and ends with one verdict: RELEASE, HOLD or REVIEW. Every source, correction and cent spent is listed.

What it checks

  1. Who the supplier really is: legal name, address and register number against GLEIF and the register Telegraph returns.
  2. Sanctions and export lists: OFAC SDN and NS-CMIC, EU, UK, UN and the US BIS Entity List.
  3. The paperwork: quantities, prices and totals on the invoice against the purchase order.
  4. The website: whether the supplier's domain is reported as malicious.

Where a paid answer is missing, partial or contradicts another source, the Mind says so and checks again with a free official source. It never treats an empty answer as "clean".

What the comparison shows, honestly: both sides reached the right verdict every time. Telegraph made the evidence stronger, with direct list answers from providers instead of search snippets and fewer failed downloads. The register catches came from GLEIF, a free source, on both sides. That is why the Mind always pairs paid answers with a free second source.

A real bug it found

Asked for Siemens AG's German register number, a Telegraph provider returned the company's LEI instead. The Mind noticed the answer had the wrong shape, held the decision, recovered the real number (HRB 6684, Amtsgericht München) through GLEIF, and only then released. The provider's builder confirmed the bug in the Telegraph Discord and fixed it. See the receipt.

Try the free first step yourself

The register check that caught the Siemens trap uses GLEIF's public data. Type a company name, an LEI or a register number such as HRB 252581. This box only searches GLEIF and spends nothing. The full check runs inside the Mind.
