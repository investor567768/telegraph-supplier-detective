This is the register step that caught the Siemens mix-up. It searches GLEIF's public data by company name, LEI or register number. The full check, with sanctions lists, runs inside the Mind.

How a check works.

  1. Read the paperworkInvoice and purchase order: names, register number, address, quantities and totals.
  2. Buy the factsRegister, sanctions and website answers, bought from Telegraph through Anjoman under a spend limit.
  3. Check the answersAnything missing, partial or conflicting is checked again against a free official source.
  4. One verdictRELEASE, HOLD or REVIEW, with every source, correction and cost listed. An empty answer is never called clean.
